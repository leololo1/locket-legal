# Locket Privacy Policy

Effective: October 8, 2026. Last updated: October 10, 2026.

Reo Morishige (森重 玲生, the "Developer") describes below how information is handled in Locket (the "App").

## 1. Overview

The App creates widgets from photo cutouts, text, and handwritten drawings. Photos are processed and designs are stored on your device. The App has no account registration feature or feature that sends photos, materials, or designs to a server operated by the Developer.

The App includes Google AdMob banner ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for advertising consent. A paid ad-free option is planned and cannot currently be purchased.

## 2. Information used on your device

The App handles the following information locally to provide its features:

- Photos you select or take, and cropped or cutout images, to create, display, and edit materials.
- Handwritten drawings and stroke data, to display, edit, and reuse drawings.
- Material and folder names, text, and design settings such as position, color, and style, to manage materials and edit or reopen designs.
- Widget images and settings, to display designs on home and lock screens.
- Device motion, such as tilt, while displaying supported effects, to vary reflections. Motion is not saved as an ongoing history or sent to the Developer.

The Developer cannot view or obtain the photos, materials, or designs stored in the App on your device.

## 3. Storage and device backups

App data is stored on your device. Widget images and settings are also stored in an App Group area shared by the App and its widget extension on the same device. This sharing does not send information to the Developer.

The App has no independent cloud sync or online backup feature. Device backups using services such as iCloud, and synchronization of exported photos through iCloud Photos, may occur according to your device settings and Apple's features. The Developer has no access to those backups or your photo library.

## 4. Photo, camera, and motion permissions

Photos are imported using the operating system's photo picker; the App processes photos you select. The camera is used when you choose to take a photo, and access to add images to the photo library is used when you choose to save an exported design. Device tilt is used for supported material effects.

Permissions are requested through the operating system and can be changed in device settings. Denying permission may prevent the relevant feature from working.

## 5. External connections and third-party services

Photo cutouts are processed on your device. The App communicates with the Google services described below for ads and consent. No separate behavioral analytics service is integrated.

Opening the Terms of Use or this policy in the in-app browser connects to GitHub Pages. GitHub may handle IP addresses, browser information, and other access information to provide and protect the site. Photos, materials, and designs stored in the App are not sent to these pages. See [GitHub's General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Operating system diagnostics, device backups, and photo synchronization are handled according to your settings and [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/en-ww/).

## 6. Google AdMob ads and consent

The App displays banner ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK and uses the Google UMP SDK for consent and privacy choices depending on your region and circumstances. Ads appear within the App's interface; they are not included in home screen or lock screen widgets, or images saved or shared from the App.

Depending on SDK settings, your consent, and operating system permissions, information such as IP addresses, device and app information and identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostics may be sent to Google. Approximate location may be inferred from an IP address. This information is used for advertising delivery and measurement, fraud prevention, service maintenance and improvement, and consent management. The App does not pass photos, cutout materials, text or design contents, or device motion to the advertising SDK.

The current implementation requests non-personalized ads and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission or permission to access the IDFA. Publisher first-party ID personalization is disabled. Non-personalized ads still involve information needed for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.

At launch, the App requests an updated consent status from UMP and displays a consent form when required. The advertising SDK starts only when UMP indicates that ads can be requested. When privacy choices are required, Google's privacy options can be opened through **Settings → Support & Legal → Ad Privacy Choices**. Changing consent or settings does not necessarily delete information already transmitted.

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en)
- [Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure for iOS](https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy/data-disclosure)
- [Personalized and non-personalized ads](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/7676680?hl=en)

## 7. Purchase information for the planned ad-free option

Purchases are planned to be processed through Apple's App Store in a version that introduces the ad-free option. Apple handles payment information; the Developer does not obtain credit card numbers. The App is expected to handle product identifiers and transaction or entitlement information needed to enable the option, verify purchase status, and restore purchases.

The conditions of the option will be shown before purchase. Disabling ads does not delete advertising information already transmitted or stop Apple's purchase processing, device backups, or similar services. Before purchases are introduced, this policy will be updated to describe the actual implementation's storage and transmission of purchase information.

## 8. Saving and sharing images

When you choose to save or share an image, it is passed to the photo library or the sharing destination you select. You and the chosen service are responsible for managing exported images and their use at the destination.

## 9. Support inquiries

If you contact us by email, your email address, name if provided, inquiry contents, and attachments are handled to respond and resolve the issue. They are retained for the time needed to handle the inquiry, and information no longer needed is deleted appropriately. Please do not send unnecessary personal information or photos.

Inquiry information is not provided to third parties except as required by law. The email service provider handles information under its own policies.

## 10. Deleting information

You can delete materials and designs using the App's deletion features. When you remove the App, local data is handled according to the operating system. The widget shared area, device backups, exported images in Photos, and images at sharing destinations may need to be managed or deleted separately using their own settings and features.

To request access, correction, deletion, or other applicable rights concerning personal information provided in a support inquiry, contact us below. We will verify your identity and respond under applicable law.

## 11. Security

The App is designed around local processing and storage and avoids unnecessary external transmission. Please also protect your device with a screen lock and operating system updates, and manage your backups and exported images.

## 12. Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated when features or information handling change. Significant changes will be announced in the App or on this page. Where consent is legally required, it will be obtained appropriately.
